After the flight, the seeds were sent to the southern Forest Service station in Gulfport, Mississippi, and to the western station in Placerville, California, with the intent to germinate them.[5] Nearly all the seeds germinated successfully, and after a few years, the Forest Service had about 420 seedlings.
